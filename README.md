# Rabbit-and-Humane-Why-Can-t-These-AI-Gadgets-Be-Mobile-Apps-


<p style="text-align: justify;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">With Rabbit and Humane touting their shiny new gadgets as the next big thing, a lot of people have a simple question: why can&rsquo;t these just be an app?</span></p>
<p class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">Turns out, a lot is happening under the hood. As reviews for the new devices have started trickling out, people are starting to ask some real questions.</span></p>

<h2 style="text-align: justify;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><span style="color:#2e75b5;font-size:13pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;">What Do These AI Devices Do?</span></h2>

<p class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><br></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">The idea behind these devices is very simple. These AI-powered assistants are marketed as voice-controlled devices that try to act as a second brain. They allow you to do most of the things you can already do with your phone like making calls, responding to messages, calling an Uber, and more.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">However, where they promise to do things differently is by the way you interact with the device.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">With the Humane AI Pin, you can activate it with a single touch and voice your query. The Humane Pin processes your query using its servers and sends a response that you can hear out loud. With this device, you can also hold out your hand, and a small projector will project a display onto your hand.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">The Rabbit R1 is a little less glorious than the Humane AI Pin, and that&apos;s by design. The devices cost much less and feature a design that most people already are familiar with. The front has a screen with a scroll wheel for navigation and an 8-megapixel camera.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">The major difference between the two is that the Humane pin costs $700 and requires a subscription whereas the Rabbit R1 costs $200 and doesn&rsquo;t need a subscription. Considering their prices, people just have one question.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><br></p>
<h2 style="text-align: justify;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><span style="color:#2e75b5;font-size:13pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;">Do We Really Need AI Gadgets?</span></h2>

<p class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><br></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">The two AI gadgets don&apos;t sound that appealing to most people. They both boast about doing things that apps on your phone already excel in doing.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">In my testing, I used a cellular connection using a SIM card. However, the response times are awful with these gadgets as it takes anywhere between 5- 30 seconds to get a response. And even after that, the responses I got were wrong.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">I thought WiFi might fix the problem. But even with a fast WiFi connection, the response time was around 10 seconds. After a quick call with a representative on the&nbsp;</span><a href="https://www.localcabledeals.com/xfinity/customer-service"><u><span style="color:#0563c1;font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;">Xfinity customer service number</span></u></a><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">, I realized that the problem was with the delay between the server and the device and not a problem with the connection.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">Even with the connection fixed, AI currently has an accuracy problem. Both Humane and Rabbit devices frequently fail to deliver the correct responses. Although this is a problem that can be fixed with time, for a new startup, these types of problems can also be product-killers.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;">And, all of these problems made me wonder.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><br></p>
<h2 style="text-align: justify;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><span style="color:#2e75b5;font-size:13pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;">Can&rsquo;t This Just Be an App?</span></h2>

<p class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><br></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">Apps like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Bing have demonstrated that most capabilities these AI products demonstrate proudly can be brilliantly executed as apps on your smartphone.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">Turns out, these ARE just apps running on an Android-based system.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;">Shocking, isn&rsquo;t it?</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;">No, not really.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">The Rabbit R1 is an AI handheld that runs a skin of Android under the hood.&nbsp;</span><a href="https://www.androidauthority.com/rabbit-r1-is-an-android-app-3438805/"><u><span style="color:#0563c1;font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;">Android Authority</span></u></a><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">&nbsp;demonstrated this by turning a Google Pixel 6a into a Rabbit R1. Although some of the features were missing, this clearly showed that the functionality of the Rabbit device could easily be done on a relatively modern smartphone.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">In the video, Mishaal Rahman showed that he could use voice commands to interact with the app, with the app responding to his queries.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">Similarly, the Humane AI Pin also runs on Android, which means that that software running on the AI pin can be modded and run on an Android phone. However, since the pin has a different interface, this can prove to be a little different compared to injecting the Rabbit OS code on Android.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;">Jesse Lyu, the company&rsquo;s CEO responded by giving a statement. He essentially denied that the Rabbit R1 is an Android app. Jesse explained that the rabbit OS and the LAM run on their specialized cloud service using low-level firmware modifications on a bespoke AOSP.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;">Jesse also warned that using a modified APK carries a lot of risks since developers can inject their code to steal data.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">However, since it has been demonstrated, there isn&rsquo;t any going back from this as it has been proven that the company can develop a dedicated launcher app for Android which will essentially do the same things as the device.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover">This has left a lot of people scratching their heads and asking a question.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><br></p>
<h2 style="text-align: justify;" class="sweezy-custom-cursor-default-hover"><span style="color:#2e75b5;font-size:13pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;">So, What&rsquo;s Next?</span></h2>
<p><br></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;">For now, the attempts to create a new category of AI-powered devices have been dead in the water. Both Humane AI Pin and Rabbit R1 are plagued with issues, mainly a battery that doesn&apos;t last long, long query times, and overheating.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;">However, we expect to see a new iteration of devices that fix these issues by implementing a better battery, on-device LLM processing, and improved performance.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;">If not, expect to at least see most of these features coming to your smartphone through an app.</span></p>
